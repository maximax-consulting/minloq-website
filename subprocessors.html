Sub-processors

The current list of third-party services Minloq uses to deliver the platform · Maintained under clause 5.2 of the Data Processing Agreement · Last updated: 5 August 2026

What this page is

A sub-processor is a third-party service Minloq uses to deliver the platform — for example, the database our systems run on, or the provider that delivers our emails. This page is the canonical, current list. Schedule A of the Data Processing Agreement renders this list; when the two are read together, this page — with its change history below — is the living record.

Changes to this list are governed by clause 5.2 of the DPA:

"Minloq will give the Customer reasonable advance notice of the addition or replacement of a sub-processor and a reasonable period to object."

When a sub-processor is added, every active account is notified by email before it takes effect for that account. An addition is never applied to an account retroactively.

How to object or consent

Reply to your notice email, or write to hello@minloq.sg — a person reads every reply. If you object within your notice period, the change does not take effect for your account and we will contact you to discuss options. If you reply AGREE, the change takes effect for your account immediately.
