# Privacy Policy

**Version 2.0 — effective 16 July 2026** · Minloq Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202615062H)
*Supersedes the version last updated April 2026.*

### 1. Who we are
Minloq Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202615062H), 73 Ubi Road 1, #09-51, Singapore 408733,
operates the Minloq platform accessible at minloq.sg. We are committed to
protecting personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act
2012 (PDPA) of Singapore.

### 2. Data we collect
We collect information you provide when signing up and using the platform,
including your name, email address, company name and UEN; financial documents
uploaded to the platform (bank statements, invoices, receipts); the content of
messages you send to the platform via WhatsApp; and usage data such as log files
and session information.

Documents you submit may contain personal data belonging to your staff,
customers and suppliers. Where you use our corporate-secretarial,
know-your-client or payroll features, we may collect identification data
including NRIC or FIN numbers and other categories set out in our
[Data Processing Agreement](/dpa), which also states which services are active
and which are not yet released.

### 3. How we use your data
Your data is used solely to provide the Minloq service — to process documents,
generate compliance outputs and operate the platform. We do not sell your data
to third parties. We do not use your data to train third-party or foundation AI
models. Where the platform improves its suggestions based on your own prior
categorisations, that learning is applied only within your own account.

### 4. Data storage and security
Your books, uploaded documents and primary AI processing are stored and
processed in Singapore (Supabase Singapore region; Amazon Web Services
ap-southeast-1; application serverless functions in Vercel's Singapore region).
Certain ancillary services — transactional email, subscription billing, SMS
verification codes, rate limiting, and WhatsApp message transport — are operated
by global providers that process limited data outside Singapore; these are listed
in Schedule A of our [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa). Each subscriber's data is
isolated using row-level security. Access is restricted to authorised personnel
only. Documents are stored in private, access-controlled storage.

### 5. Data retention
We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as required by
Singapore law. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at
any time by emailing hello@minloq.sg. We will process deletion requests within
30 days, subject to any retention required by law — including statutory
accounting and tax record-keeping obligations, which under IRAS guidance are
generally five years and which may override a deletion request. Our platform uses
a soft-delete model that preserves the audit trail and does not hard-delete
accounting records.

### 6. Third-party processors
We use the third-party processors listed in Schedule A of our
[Data Processing Agreement](/dpa), which is the authoritative and current list.
It covers document processing and storage, database and file storage,
application hosting, WhatsApp messaging, transactional email, subscription
billing, SMS verification, rate limiting, and website form handling. Each entry
states its purpose and processing location. All processors are engaged under
appropriate data protection terms.

### 7. Your rights
Under the PDPA, you have the right to access personal data we hold about you,
correct inaccurate personal data, and withdraw consent for data processing
(subject to legal or contractual restrictions). To exercise these rights,
contact us at hello@minloq.sg.

### 8. Cookies
We use only essential cookies necessary to operate the platform (session
management and authentication). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

### 9. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Each version carries a version
number and effective date. Where a change is material, we will ask you to review
and accept the updated policy when you next sign in to the platform, and we will
record your acceptance.

### 10. Contact
For any privacy-related queries or to exercise your rights, contact our Data
Protection Officer at hello@minloq.sg.
