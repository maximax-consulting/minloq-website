FAQ

Straight answers.

Is Minloq an accounting or tax advisor?+
No. Minloq is a productivity tool. It prepares and checks routine work; your accountant remains responsible for all filings, and Minloq does not provide tax, accounting, or legal advice.
What does “AI-assisted compilation” mean?+
Outputs are compilations prepared from information provided by management. No audit or review is performed by Minloq. Your accountant remains responsible for accuracy.
Where is my data hosted?+
In Singapore, on AWS ap-southeast-1, with row-level isolation between tenants. Your data is never used to train AI models, and handling is PDPA compliant.
Which bots are available today?+
Two: Ben for bookkeeping and document intake, and Gary for GST checking. Those are the only bots live right now.
Do you support the Cash Accounting Scheme?+
No. Gary works on the accrual basis — supplies are counted in the period of the invoice date. Minloq does not support IRAS's Cash Accounting Scheme.
Do I need a credit card for the trial?+
No. The trial runs 14 days with no credit card required.
